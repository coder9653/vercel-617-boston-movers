Life in Rhode Island

Why Make The Move From Boston To Rhode Island?

A Change Of Pace

Rhode Island offers urban neighbourhoods, suburbs and coastal towns. Depending on where you settle, everyday life may feel quite different from Boston.

Housing & Everyday Costs

Housing and day-to-day costs vary between Rhode Island communities. Compare rents, property prices, utilities and transport to see how your budget might change.

Beaches & Fresh Air

Food & Culture

FAQ

Rhode Island Moving Questions

How long does a move from Boston to Rhode Island take?

It depends on your destination, property size, inventory and services. Collection, loading, travel and unloading all add to the time. Send us your addresses to discuss timing.

Do I have to be there for the whole move?

It helps to have someone available at collection and delivery to give access and any important instructions. If you can't be there throughout, talk to us about alternatives in advance.

What won't professional movers take?

Some hazardous, flammable or restricted items may not be suitable for transport. If you're unsure about something, ask before packing it.

Do you offer packing and unpacking?

Packing help can be arranged when you book. Tell us which belongings need help and how much support you'd like.

How do you protect furniture?

Protective moving blankets help reduce scratches and marks. Let us know about delicate or valuable pieces before moving day.

Can you take apart and rebuild beds and dressers?

Yes, for selected items. Just point out which pieces when we discuss your move.

Will you arrange furniture in my new home?

Furniture placement and rearranging can be part of your service — tell us where larger items should go.
