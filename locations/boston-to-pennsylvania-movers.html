Before you move

Things Worth Considering Before Your Move

Moving to another state is a great opportunity to plan ahead. Alongside booking movers, think about the details that could affect your move and your first few weeks in Pennsylvania.

Give Yourself Enough Time

Your timeline depends on property size, distance, the services you need and the agreed delivery plan. Confirm dates early and leave enough time for packing, preparation and access arrangements.

Look Into Living Costs

Costs vary a lot across the state — housing, utilities, transport and everyday spending can differ between Philadelphia, Pittsburgh, smaller cities and rural areas. Research your new area to set a sensible budget.

Be Ready For The Seasons

Expect cold winters, snow in some areas and warm summers. Weather can affect travel and moving arrangements, particularly in winter, so check the forecast and keep your essentials close.

FAQ

Boston To Pennsylvania FAQ

How are Pennsylvania moving costs calculated?

We look at the size of your move, how far you're going, how much you're moving and any extra services. Packing, furniture prep and access can also affect the estimate.

How long does the move take?

Delivery time depends on your destination, the size of the move, travel arrangements and other logistics. Send us your collection and delivery details to discuss timing.

Can you supply packing materials?

We can discuss packing when you book. Let us know which items need help and whether you need materials.

Can you move my furniture from a Boston apartment to a Pennsylvania house?

Yes. We handle moves between apartments, houses and other properties — just share details about your furniture, access and destination.

Do you take furniture apart and put it back together?

Yes, this can be part of your move. Point out which pieces need it when we discuss your plans.

Do you go to cities other than Philadelphia?

Yes. Our listed destinations include Philadelphia, Pittsburgh, Allentown, Reading, Erie, Harrisburg, Lancaster, Scranton and several others. Contact us about your exact destination.

What should I do before moving day?

Make an inventory, organise essentials, confirm access at both properties and flag anything that needs special handling. Confirming the date and services ahead of time helps too.

Will you help place furniture in the new house?

Yes, furniture can be arranged as part of your service — tell the team where larger items should go.
