Before you move

Getting Set For Life In Connecticut

An interstate move is a chance to plan ahead. As well as booking your movers, think about anything that could affect the move and your first few weeks in Connecticut.

Plan A Realistic Schedule

Your timeline depends on property size, distance, services and delivery arrangements. Confirm dates early and allow plenty of time for packing, prep and access.

Check The Cost Of Living

Costs can differ a lot within Connecticut — housing, utilities, transport and everyday spending vary between bigger cities, coastal towns and quieter communities. Research your area so you can budget properly.

Get Ready For The Weather

Connecticut sees cold winters, snow in some places and warm summers. Weather can affect travel and moving, especially in winter, so check the forecast and keep essentials within easy reach.

Boston To Connecticut Questions

What does a move from Boston to Connecticut cost?

How long does it take?

That depends on your destination, property size, inventory and delivery arrangements. A move to Hartford can differ considerably from one to a coastal town or another part of the state. Contact us to discuss your schedule.

How should I get ready for my Connecticut move?

Start by organising your belongings and deciding what's going. Pack essentials separately, flag fragile or oversized furniture and confirm access and parking at both properties. Agreeing your date and services early helps too.

Do you protect furniture during the move?

Yes, furniture protection can be included. We use protective moving blankets to help reduce scratches and marks.

Do you move to Stamford, Hartford and New Haven?

Can you help with packing and unpacking?

Can you move my business to Connecticut?

Yes. We can discuss office furniture, workplace equipment, collection and delivery. Share your inventory and schedule so we understand the job.

Can you take apart and rebuild big furniture?
