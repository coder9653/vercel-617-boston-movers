Heading From Boston To New York? We'll Take Care Of The Trip

People make the Boston-to-New York leap for all sorts of reasons: a job offer, a business that's outgrown its space, a fresh start with family, or simply wanting somewhere new. Whatever yours is, shifting a home or office between two crowded metro areas only works with good planning, the right vehicle and belongings that are handled with care.

Our long-distance service connects Boston and its neighbouring Massachusetts towns with New York City and with communities right across New York State.

Apartments, houses or offices — we can look after the important parts of the job. That includes protecting furniture, helping you pack, loading, driving, unloading, taking furniture apart and putting it back together, and setting your things down where they belong.

Got a one-bedroom or a full house? Either way, the plan we put together reflects what you own, when you need to move and where you're going.

Two Busy Cities, One Organised Move

Booking a truck is the easy part. Boston and New York both come with heavy traffic, apartment buildings that enforce access rules, patchy parking and properties where moving arrangements have to be made well ahead.

On the Boston side you might be dealing with a cramped staircase, a shared front door or barely any room to load. On the New York side there may be building management to contact, a service elevator to reserve or a fixed window for deliveries.

Each of these affects pickup and drop-off, so sorting them out early takes a lot of uncertainty out of moving day.

We'll ask about access at both ends, how much you're moving, when you'd like to go and what help you want. With that, we can talk through transport and preparation before anything is loaded.

You handle the big decisions; we'll help with the practical side while your belongings are made ready for the road.

Moving services

Help For Renters, Homeowners And Businesses

A furnished studio, a family house with several bedrooms and a company relocating its equipment all call for different levels of support.

Here's how we can help with a Boston-to-New York relocation.

Home Relocations

Furniture, appliances, wardrobes full of clothes, kitchen gear, electronics, keepsakes — every item has to be prepared and carried in a way that suits its size and condition.

Whether it's an apartment, a condo or a house, we can protect furniture, sort out packing, load the truck, drive it and unload at your new place.

Send us a rough inventory and your destination and we'll recommend the arrangements that fit.

Business Moves

Taking a company to New York means organising furniture, office equipment, supplies and everything else the team uses day to day.

We can transport desks, chairs, storage units and suitable equipment, and we'll talk about how to pack and handle anything that needs extra care.

From compact offices to larger commercial floors, the schedule is built around how your business operates.

Taking Furniture Apart & Rebuilding It

Bulky pieces often refuse to fit through narrow doors, tight stairwells and apartment hallways. Breaking them down first makes the job much easier.

Our crew can dismantle beds, tables, wardrobes and other larger items before they travel and rebuild them once delivered.

Everything can then go into the rooms you choose, as agreed beforehand.

How it works

Step By Step: Boston To New York

When everyone knows what happens before, during and after the drive, moves go more smoothly. We start with your requirements, then prepare your belongings and coordinate delivery.

  1. 01

    Send Us The Basics

    Let us know where we're collecting from around Boston, where you're going in New York, how big the property is and the date you have in mind.

    We'll then go over how much you're moving, access, packing and any extras.

  2. 02

    Get Things Ready To Travel

    Depending on what you've chosen, furniture is wrapped, fragile items are packed and larger pieces are broken down before loading.

    Protective materials make sure everything is ready to be handled.

  3. 03

    Pack The Truck Carefully

    Furniture, boxes and other belongings are arranged inside the vehicle.

    Each item is placed and secured so it moves as little as possible on the journey.

  4. 04

    Drive To New York

    With loading done, your shipment leaves Massachusetts for your New York address.

    The route, traffic, collection arrangements and delivery slot all influence timing, and we'll explain the plan when you book.

  5. 05

    Unload & Settle In

    We carry everything into your new place, rebuild furniture where agreed and position it the way you want.

    Taking away moving-related packing materials and a post-move tidy can be included, though this isn't a professional house clean.

FAQ

Questions About Moving From Boston To New York

What will a Boston to New York move cost me?

Pricing reflects how big the shipment is, how many items you have, the transport required and which services you add.

A studio is priced differently from a large home or an office, so send 617 Boston Movers your details for a proper estimate.

How long does the move take?

It hinges on your addresses, the shipment, traffic and the schedule. Route information gives the road distance from Boston to New York City as roughly 217 miles via I-90.

Share your preferred dates and we'll explain the expected collection and delivery.

Can you bring packing materials?

Yes. Moving blankets, boxes, tape, bubble wrap and other protective supplies can be arranged along with your move.

Do you move artwork and fragile pieces?

We do, with extra preparation and careful handling. Mention mirrors, paintings, antiques, glassware or delicate electronics when you request an estimate.

Is there storage available?

Yes, if there's a gap between leaving Boston and taking delivery in New York. Contact us to talk it through.

Will you move a whole house to New York?

Absolutely — apartments and multi-bedroom homes alike. The estimate depends on what you're moving, access and services.

Do you move offices?

Yes. Speak to 617 Boston Movers about your furniture, equipment, packing and delivery.

Can the crew dismantle and rebuild furniture?

Yes. Suitable pieces can be taken apart for the journey and reassembled when they arrive.

Can I travel with the movers in the truck?

Please arrange separate travel for yourself and your family. Our team is happy to answer questions about the transport side of your move.
