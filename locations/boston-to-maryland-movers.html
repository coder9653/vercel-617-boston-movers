Before moving day

Smart Checks Before Your Move To Maryland

Moves between Boston and Maryland can involve different property layouts, building rules and access arrangements. A little prep makes pickup and delivery far more straightforward.

Access & Parking

Boston properties may have narrow stairs, shared entrances, limited street parking or set moving hours, while Maryland apartments and communities may have rules for delivery vehicles.

Ask both property managers about access, parking, loading zones and reservations.

Declutter First

Decide which furniture, appliances, boxes and personal items you're taking. An organised inventory helps estimate shipment size and the right arrangements.

Set aside anything you plan to donate, sell or discard.

Fragile & Oversized Items

Artwork, mirrors, glass furniture, large appliances and delicate belongings may need extra prep — mention them when requesting an estimate.

Property Dates

Confirm when you'll have access to the new address, and plan around lease dates, closings, key collection and any delivery-time limits.

Essentials Box

Where it makes sense, keep important documents, daily medication, valuables, chargers and essentials apart from the main shipment for an easier first few days.
