Who we are

Moving Help For Boston And Beyond

Based at 215 Condor Street, 617 Boston Movers serves the Boston metro area and destinations further afield with fast, affordable moving.

Our mobile crew means you choose the time and place for your service. From the first call to the last box, the aim is the same: a move that runs smoothly and without hassle.
